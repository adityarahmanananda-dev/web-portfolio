Book-O-Worm

Your bookshelf, photographed and searchable. Scan an ISBN barcode, pull the metadata automatically, and keep every PDF or EPUB on your own device.

  • ✓On-device EAN-13 barcode scan plus OCR fallback for the ISBN.
  • ✓Open Library autofill for title, authors, publisher, pages, subjects, and covers.
  • ✓Multi-category filters, duplicate-ISBN guard, ratings, and CSV export.
  • ✓Private local storage, offline ZIP backup, and “open with” any reader app.
